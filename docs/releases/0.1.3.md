Audio is now saved incrementally while recording, reducing the risk of losing a long capture at Stop.

- Added bounded recording queues and live saved-progress display.
- Preserved committed audio from interrupted sessions for original-format export.
- Added Original export without decoding or conversion, recommended for long recordings.
- Reported download completion and export failures explicitly.
- Protected recording sessions from stale capture messages.

Validated with a real 20-minute Firefox recording: 1,201 chunks, 19.8 MB, successful decoding and download. All 79 unit tests passed. Restart recovery was tested by clearing session state and reloading the extension; this was not an OS-crash test.

Existing export preferences are preserved. WAV/MP3 conversion still decodes the entire recording and can use substantial memory. Interrupted files may require repair before playback.

Install and receive signed updates through [Firefox Add-ons](https://addons.mozilla.org/firefox/addon/tab-audio-rec/). Mozilla review and publication may follow after submission.
